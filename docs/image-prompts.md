# Imagens conceituais

## Recorte transparente da e-bike

Arquivo final usado na abertura e nos pôsteres: `public/images/ebike-cutout.webp`. Edição realizada com o tool integrado imagegen a partir da imagem de abertura, preservando o canal alfa no WebP. O arquivo `ebike-concept.webp` mantém a versão de estúdio como alternativa.

Prompt de edição completo:

> Edit this product photograph. Remove the entire studio background and floor and output the exact same electric bicycle as a clean photorealistic cutout with a genuinely transparent alpha background. Keep the entire bicycle unchanged: exact geometry, matte forest green frame, tan sidewall tires, rack, handlebars, components and lighting. Preserve both complete wheels, do not crop anything. Remove background between wheel spokes and inside the frame too. No text, no new objects. Keep only a subtle semi-transparent contact shadow if feasible; no opaque backdrop or checkerboard pattern. The output must have actual transparency, for direct placement on a light cream website.


Criadas com o tool integrado imagegen (skill imagegen), sem CLI/API adicional. Convertidas para WebP com FFmpeg para uso local no site. As imagens representam conceitos ilustrativos, não uma frota ou participantes já existentes.

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: wide editorial lifestyle photograph for an electric bicycle urban mobility website. Two young adult Brazilian office commuters, a woman with medium brown skin and a man, wearing well fitting bicycle helmets and everyday olive and cream casual clothing with backpacks, riding elegant dark green urban electric bicycles on a protected paved bike lane in a leafy mid-size southern Brazilian city. Candid side view, cycling left to right, natural relaxed expressions, physically accurate full bicycles, both hands on handlebars. Lush mature trees and modest architecture, early morning sunlight, soft film grain, natural warm highlights and forest green shadows. Sophisticated Kinfolk outdoor editorial photography, authentic and approachable, not racing or sportswear. Wide 3:2 framing with riders centered in the middle-right, foliage blurred at edges, no text, no logos, no watermarks. Illustrative scene for an early-stage project.
